📚 Complete Metrics Guide & Benchmarks (Click to expand/collapse)

🏢 Company Identifiers

Ticker: Unique stock exchange symbol.

Company Name: Registered corporate entity name.

Sector: Broad economic sector classification.

Industry: Specific market sub-industry.

📈 Growth Metrics

Revenue Growth 1Y: YoY top-line expansion. Benchmark: >10%

Revenue CAGR 3Y: 3-year compound revenue rate. Benchmark: >15%

EPS Growth 1Y: YoY diluted earnings per share growth. Benchmark: >20%

EPS CAGR 3Y: 3-year compound EPS growth power. Benchmark: >20%

💰 Profitability & Capital Efficiency

Gross Margin: Gross profit / revenue. Benchmark: >40%

Operating Margin: EBIT / revenue (operating efficiency). Benchmark: >20%

Net Margin: Net income / revenue (bottom line). Benchmark: >15%

Op Margin Trend: Recent margin expansion or contraction. Benchmark: >0%

ROE: Return on shareholder equity. Benchmark: >15%–20%

ROIC: Return on invested capital (economic moat). Benchmark: >12%–15%

💵 Cash Flow Quality

FCF/NI: Free cash flow to net income conversion. Benchmark: >0.8

FCF Margin: Free cash flow as % of revenue. Benchmark: >15%

FCF Growth 1Y: YoY free cash flow growth. Benchmark: >10%

🏦 Financial Health & Solvency

Net Debt/EBITDA: Leverage ratio. Safe: <2.0 | Negative = Net Cash balance

📊 Valuation

P/E Ratio: Price to earnings multiple. Value: <15 | Fair: 15–25 | Growth: 25–40
